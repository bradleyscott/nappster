Nappster · design prototypes

Three sleeps, three rooms.

Three redesign concepts for the Nappster dashboard, each built with the Hallmark anti-slop skill — a different macrostructure, theme, and genre per concept, all carrying the same app: buttons-first logging, a state hero, and a timeline. Sample moment: Tuesday 2:28 pm — Luna napping, asleep since 1:04 pm.

A · The Sun & Moon

playful · Stat-Led · Hum

Cream paper, pear / cyan / coral multi-accent, rounded sans. The countdown is a giant counter that ticks up, the moon breathes, logging a nap fires a coral star-burst. For parents who want the app to feel alive.

Open →

B · The Quiet Card

modern-minimal · Bento Grid · Coral

Warm-grey paper, one restrained coral accent, Geist throughout. A bento of tiles — state, tonight, naps, actions, timeline — with hairline borders and no bounce. For parents who want the app to stay out of the way.

Open →

C · The Sleep Daybook

editorial · Long Document · Newsprint

Warm cream, Playfair + Crimson serifs, hairlines instead of cards. The timeline is the day's record, written like a journal with marginal notes. Calm by design — a sleep log that reads like a broadsheet.
